Imbalance Handling Comparison
============================

Best Strategy : Baseline

Precision : 0.7812
Recall    : 0.7353
F1 Score  : 0.7576

Three strategies were evaluated:
1. Baseline Random Forest
2. Random Forest with class_weight='balanced'
3. Random Forest with SMOTE oversampling

The selected strategy achieved the highest F1 Score while maintaining a good balance between precision and recall. Since F1 Score combines both metrics into a single measure, it is the most appropriate metric for comparing performance on moderately imbalanced datasets.
